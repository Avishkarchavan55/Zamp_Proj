# X4 · Email intake: Lokseva Pashukhadya

**Vendor:** Lokseva Pashukhadya Private Limited  
**Channel:** Email

## What it tests
A vendor who only emails documents (no cheque). The app reads the attachments, builds the application, then the vendor confirms the details and adds the cheque through a link.

## How to run it
1. From any email account, send **one email** to the onboarding inbox (acmevendoronboarding@gmail.com) with subject `Vendor registration - Lokseva Pashukhadya` and attach the **4 files** in this folder (not the `reply` folder). A short message like *"We are a registered MSME (Small). Sandeep Gaikwad, +91 98221 40576"* helps.
2. Within ~1 minute you get *Action needed* email. Open its link.
3. Check the pre-filled details, upload `reply/Lokseva_Cancelled_Cheque.png` as the bank proof, and press **Confirm**.

## Expected result
**Waiting for vendor** (confirm + cheque) → after step 3 → **Approved**.

## What to look at
The vendor's *Email* tag; the activity shows *Details filled in from documents* (bank details taken from the cheque).
