# E4 · Wrong document uploaded

**Vendor:** Kaveri Freight and Logistics Pvt Ltd  
**Channel:** Vendor portal

## What it tests
The vendor put their GST certificate in the bank-proof slot. AI recognises the document type and asks for the right one.

## How to run it
**Submit on the vendor portal** (`/apply`).

- **Quick way:** open *Staff tool: fill this form with a sample vendor* (visible only when signed in to the Company portal) → pick **Wrong document uploaded**. It fills every field and attaches the documents. Then press **Submit application**.
- **By hand:** type the values below and attach the files in this folder.

**Then:** After the run, open the link in the request email (or the vendor's page) and upload `resubmission/Kaveri_Cancelled_Cheque.png` from this folder as the bank proof.

## Expected result
**Waiting for vendor** → after you upload the cheque → **Approved**.

## What to look at
Watch the journey tracker on the vendor's page: request sent → resubmitted → re-checked → approved.

## Files
- Certificate of Incorporation → `Kaveri_Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf`
- GST Registration Certificate → `Kaveri_GST_REG-06.pdf`
- PAN Card → `Kaveri_PAN_Card.png`
- Cancelled cheque or bank letter → `Kaveri_Bank_Proof.pdf`
