# E6 · MSME category mismatch

**Vendor:** Pragati Polymers Pvt Ltd  
**Channel:** Vendor portal

## What it tests
The vendor declares *Micro* enterprise (which legally requires payment within 45 days), but the Udyam certificate says *Medium*.

## How to run it
**Submit on the vendor portal** (`/apply`).

- **Quick way:** open *Demo: fill this form with a test vendor* → pick **MSME category mismatch**. It fills every field and attaches the documents. Then press **Submit application**.
- **By hand:** type the values below and attach the files in this folder.

## Expected result
**Needs your decision**: *MSME category doesn't match certificate* (it decides the payment terms).

## What to look at
The review page explains why it matters: MSMED Act s.15 and Income-tax s.43B(h).
