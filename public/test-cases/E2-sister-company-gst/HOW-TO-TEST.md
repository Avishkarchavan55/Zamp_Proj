# E2 · Sister-company GST certificate

**Vendor:** Northstar Industrial Supplies Pvt Ltd  
**Channel:** Vendor portal

## What it tests
Every field is valid on its own, but the GST certificate belongs to a sister company with a near-identical name (Solutions vs Supplies) and a different PAN.

## How to run it
**Submit on the vendor portal** (`/apply`).

- **Quick way:** open *Demo: fill this form with a test vendor* → pick **Sister-company GST certificate**. It fills every field and attaches the documents. Then press **Submit application**.
- **By hand:** type the values below and attach the files in this folder.

## Expected result
**Needs your decision**: *GSTIN belongs to another company* and *Company name doesn't match documents*.

## What to look at
The review page shows the form value next to what the GST certificate says, with the document highlighted.

## Files
- Certificate of Incorporation → `Northstar_Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf`
- GST Registration Certificate → `Northstar_GST_REG-06.pdf`
- PAN Card → `Northstar_PAN_Card.png`
