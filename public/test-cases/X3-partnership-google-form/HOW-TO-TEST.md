# X3 · Partnership firm (Google Form)

**Vendor:** Riverside Traders  
**Channel:** Google Form

## What it tests
A partnership firm applying through the Google Form. No incorporation certificate is needed for a firm.

## How to run it
**Submit through the Google Form** (link on the *Become a supplier* page, `/supplier`). Choose *Partnership Firm*, *MSME registered: No*, *MSME category: Not applicable*, type the values below and upload the files.

## Expected result
**Approved**, with a *Google Form* tag on the vendor.

## What to look at
*All vendors* shows the Google Form tag; the checks are exactly the same as on the portal.

## Files
- GST Registration Certificate → `Riverside_GST_REG-06.pdf`
- PAN Card → `Riverside_PAN_Card.png`
- Cancelled cheque or bank letter → `Riverside_Cancelled_Cheque.png`
